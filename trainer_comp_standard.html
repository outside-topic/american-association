Overview

Framework Purpose

The ASTC Trainer Competency Framework (TCF) defines the professional standards required for effective training delivery, instructional design, learning facilitation, assessment, and workforce capability development.

This Framework Supports

Certification design and assessment
Membership eligibility and progression
Trainer accreditation and quality assurance
Employer competency validation
CPD planning and benchmarking
Global professional recognition
Career Progression

Proficiency Levels

A clear pathway defining expectations at every stage of professional development.

L1

Foundation Trainer

Applies basic delivery methods under guidance

L2

Practitioner Trainer

Delivers independently with consistent quality

L3

Advanced Trainer

Designs programs and adapts to complex environments

L4

Expert Trainer

Leads programs, mentors others, drives impact

L5

Master Trainer

Defines standards, publishes, influences industry

Core Standards

Competency Domains

Ten essential domains defining the complete skill set of a modern training professional.

1. Professional Ethics & Safeguarding

Clusters
Ethical facilitation Learner protection Data privacy Fair assessment Professional boundaries
Indicators
  • Applies ASTC ethics in delivery
  • Creates psychologically safe environments
  • Protects learner data
  • Handles conflicts responsibly

2. Communication & Facilitation Presence

Clusters
Verbal clarity and structure Engagement and storytelling Group dynamics management Feedback delivery Cross-cultural sensitivity
Indicators
  • Maintains learner engagement
  • Handles difficult participants professionally
  • Communicates complex ideas clearly

3. Adult Learning Science

Clusters
Andragogy principles Cognitive learning design Motivation and engagement theory Experiential learning Transfer of learning
Indicators
  • Selects methods based on learning science
  • Designs practice and reinforcement

4. Training Needs Analysis & Consulting

Clusters
Stakeholder analysis Performance gap diagnosis Data gathering Solution scoping ROI alignment
Indicators
  • Differentiates training vs performance issues
  • Aligns interventions to business KPIs

5. Learning Design & Curriculum Development

Clusters
Competency-based design Learning outcomes mapping Content sequencing Activity design Inclusivity and accessibility
Indicators
  • Produces structured curricula and manuals
  • Designs measurable objectives

6. Facilitation & Delivery Excellence

Clusters
Session flow management Engagement techniques Classroom/virtual control Energy management Real-time adaptation
Indicators
  • Manages timing and learner interaction effectively
  • Adjusts approach dynamically

7. Assessment & Learning Evaluation

Clusters
Rubric design Practical assessments Feedback systems Evaluation models Data reporting
Indicators
  • Applies fair and reliable assessments
  • Reports learning impact

8. Digital Learning & Technology

Clusters
LMS utilization Virtual facilitation tools Digital content creation Learning analytics AI ethics
Indicators
  • Uses digital tools effectively and ethically

9. Program Quality & Continuous Improvement

Clusters
QA systems Documentation Risk management Learner feedback analysis Improvement cycles
Indicators
  • Implements structured quality reviews

10. Professional Leadership & Practice Development

Clusters
Mentoring and supervision Thought leadership Community contribution Business development CPD planning
Indicators
  • Leads professional initiatives and innovation

Evidence Requirements

L1
Knowledge test + micro delivery
L2
Full observed session + lesson plan
L3
Program design + evaluation report
L4
Multi-program leadership + mentoring
L5
Industry contribution + standards development
