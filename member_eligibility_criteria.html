Membership Eligibility Criteria

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC) offers structured membership categories designed to recognize professional growth, competence, leadership contribution, and industry impact in the fields of training, coaching, learning and development, leadership facilitation, and organizational capability development.

Membership Categories

Explore the levels awarded based on experience, qualifications, and contribution.

Level 1

Associate Member (AMASTC)

Purpose: Designed for emerging professionals, early-career practitioners, aspiring trainers/coaches, and individuals entering the learning and development profession.
Academic / Learning Qualification
  • Diploma, degree, or certification in Training & Development, Coaching, or Education.
  • Alternative Entry: Recognized certification by Govt or professional bodies.
Professional Exposure
  • Minimum 2 years in Training delivery, Coaching support, Facilitation, or Teaching.
  • Experience in HR/L&D support or Instructional assistance.
Alternative Entry
  • Having any certification in the area of Training, Education or Coaching awarded by Govt or any other recognized institutes/professional body.
Additional Requirements
  • Agreement Comply with ASTC Code of Ethics.
  • Commitment to Continuous Professional Development (CPD).
Level 2

Professional Member (PMASTC)

Purpose: Recognizes qualified professionals actively practicing in training, coaching, facilitation, learning, consulting, or organizational development roles.
Professional Experience
  • Minimum 5 years in Corporate training, Coaching, or Leadership development and Facilitation.
  • Experience in Instructional design , Organizational capability building and Learning & development .
Professional Practice
  • Evidence of active involvement in Delivering training/coaching sessions, Designing learning programs , Managing learning initiatives and Supporting organizational development.
Qualification Requirement
  • Recognized professional certification.
  • Degree or diploma in related field.
  • ASTC-recognized competency evidence.
Additional Requirements
  • Acceptance of ASTC professional standards.
  • Commitment to CPD compliance.
  • Professional profile or portfolio submission.
Level 3

Senior Professional Member (SPMASTC)

Purpose: Recognizes experienced professionals with advanced expertise, leadership exposure, strategic contribution, and significant professional practice.
Professional Experience
  • Minimum 12 years in Training & facilitation , Executive coaching, Organizational learning, Leadership development , Talent development , Consulting or workforce development;
Leadership & Strategic Contribution
  • Evidence of one or more Leading major learning initiatives, Managing training departments or academies, Developing competency frameworks , Designing strategic workforce programs, Coaching senior leaders or executives , Speaking, publishing, or industry contribution.
Professional Achievement
  • Senior professional certification, Significant organizational contribution.
  • Recognized industry achievement, ASTC competency assessment approval.
Additional Requirements
  • Strong professional reputation and Ethical standing and compliance history.
  • CPD and professional contribution evidence.
Level 4

ASTC Fellow (FASTC)

Purpose: Represents the highest honorary level of recognition awarded by ASTC to distinguished professionals who have made exceptional contributions to the profession, industry, research, leadership, or the ASTC community.

Fellowship may be awarded to individuals who demonstrate significant contribution in one or more of the following areas

Professional Leadership
  • Exceptional leadership in training, coaching, or learning professions.
  • Industry-wide influence and contribution and Executive or strategic leadership roles.
Professional Contribution
  • Development of professional standards or frameworks .
  • Research, publications, or thought leadership.
  • International speaking or advisory contribution.
  • Mentorship and community development.
Contribution to ASTC or the Profession
  • Long-term contribution to ASTC initiatives.
  • Advisory, governance, or standards contribution.
  • Advancement of ethical and professional practices.
  • Global advocacy for professional development.
Recognition & Reputation
  • Highly respected professional standing and Demonstrated ethical leadership.

Fellowship Award Process

A prestigious journey of recognition for industry excellence.

Nomination & Invitation

The process begins through formal nomination by peers or a direct invitation from the society.

Leadership Review

Applications are subject to rigorous review and approval by the ASTC leadership and governance body.

Evidence Validation

Supporting evidence, professional portfolios, or industry references may be required for final verification.

General Conditions

All ASTC members are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional integrity and ethical conduct.

  • Comply with ASTC Code of Ethics
  • Maintain professional conduct
  • Avoid credential misuse
  • Support professional standards and integrity
  • Participate in continuous professional development where applicable

Important Note

ASTC reserves the right to review, suspend, or revoke membership in cases of ethical breach, misconduct, or misrepresentation.
