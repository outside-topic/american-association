ASTC Membership Framework

ASTC Membership Eligibility Criteria

American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC)

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC) offers structured membership categories designed to recognize professional growth, competence, leadership contribution, and industry impact in the fields of training, coaching, learning and development, leadership facilitation, and organizational capability development.

Membership levels are awarded based on professional experience, qualifications, competencies, ethical commitment, and contribution to the profession.

Entry Level Membership

ASTC Associate Member (AMASTC)

Designed for emerging professionals, aspiring trainers/coaches, and individuals entering the learning and development profession.

Experience Stage

Early Career

Purpose

The Associate Member category is designed for emerging professionals, early-career practitioners, aspiring trainers/coaches, and individuals entering the learning and development profession.

Academic / Learning Qualification

Diploma, degree, certification, or professional training in:

  • Training & Development
  • Coaching
  • Education

Alternative Entry

Having any certification in the area of Training, Education or Coaching awarded by Govt or any other recognized institutes/professional body.

Professional Exposure

Minimum 2 years of involvement in:

  • Training delivery
  • Coaching support
  • Facilitation
  • Teaching
  • HR/L&D support role
  • Instructional assistance

Additional Requirements

Agreement to comply with ASTC Code of Ethics
Commitment to continuous professional development (CPD)
Submission of membership application form
Professional Practitioner

ASTC Professional Member (PMASTC)

Recognizes qualified professionals actively practicing in training, coaching, facilitation, learning, consulting, or organizational development roles.

Experience Stage

Experienced Practitioner

Professional Experience

Minimum 5 years of professional experience in one or more areas:

  • Corporate training
  • Coaching or mentoring
  • Facilitation
  • Learning & development
  • Leadership development
  • Instructional design
  • Organizational capability building

Professional Practice

Evidence of active involvement in:

  • Delivering training/coaching sessions
  • Designing learning programs
  • Managing learning initiatives
  • Supporting organizational development

Qualification Requirement

At least ONE of the following:

  • Recognized professional certification
  • Degree or diploma in related field
  • ASTC-recognized competency evidence

Additional Requirements

Acceptance of ASTC professional standards
Commitment to CPD compliance
Professional profile or portfolio submission
Strategic Leadership Level

ASTC Senior Professional Member (SPMASTC)

Recognizes experienced professionals with advanced expertise, leadership exposure, strategic contribution, and significant professional practice.

Experience Stage

Advanced Strategic Professional

Professional Experience

Minimum 12 years of professional experience in:

  • Training and facilitation
  • Executive coaching
  • Organizational learning
  • Leadership development
  • Talent development
  • Consulting or workforce development

Leadership & Strategic Contribution

Evidence of one or more:

  • Leading major learning initiatives
  • Managing training departments or academies
  • Developing competency frameworks
  • Designing strategic workforce programs
  • Coaching senior leaders or executives
  • Speaking, publishing, or industry contribution

Professional Achievement

At least ONE of the following:

  • Senior professional certification
  • Significant organizational contribution
  • Recognized industry achievement
  • ASTC competency assessment approval

Additional Requirements

Strong professional reputation
Ethical standing and compliance history
CPD and professional contribution evidence
Fellow Level

ASTC Fellow Member (FASTC)

Represents the highest honorary level of recognition awarded by ASTC to distinguished professionals who have made exceptional contributions to the profession, industry, research, leadership, or the ASTC community.

Fellowship is honorary and recognition-based.

Top Stage

Fellow Membership

Eligibility Criteria

Fellowship may be awarded to individuals who demonstrate significant contribution in one or more of the following areas

Professional Leadership

  • Exceptional leadership in training, coaching, or learning professions
  • Industry-wide influence and contribution
  • Executive or strategic leadership roles

Professional Contribution

  • Development of professional standards or frameworks
  • Research, publications, or thought leadership
  • International speaking or advisory contribution
  • Mentorship and community development

Contribution to ASTC or the Profession

  • Long-term contribution to ASTC initiatives
  • Advisory, governance, or standards contribution
  • Advancement of ethical and professional practices
  • Global advocacy for professional development

Recognition & Reputation

  • Highly respected professional standing
  • Demonstrated ethical leadership
  • Significant impact on individuals, organizations, or the profession

Suitable For

  • Industry leaders, Distinguished trainers/coaches
  • Researchers and authors, Senior consultants
  • Academic leaders, Global advisors and contributors

Fellowship Award Process

Fellowship is awarded through nomination or invitation
Subject to review and approval by ASTC leadership/governance body
Supporting evidence or references may be required
