American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC)

Advancing Professional Excellence in Training, Coaching, and Human Development

Excellence in Training, Coaching, and Human Development

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC) is a globally oriented professional association dedicated to elevating the standards, credibility, and professional competence of trainers, coaches, learning professionals, and organizational development practitioners.

ASTC serves as a trusted platform for competency standard, ethical governance, and continuous professional development (CPD). We bring together educators, corporate trainers, executive coaches, facilitators, consultants, instructional designers, and learning leaders from diverse industries to strengthen the future of professional learning and human capability development.

Through internationally aligned competency frameworks, industry partnerships, and a strong ethical foundation, ASTC empowers professionals to deliver measurable learning impact, business value, and sustainable workforce transformation across organizations and communities worldwide.

Who We Are

Redefining Excellence in Human Development

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches is an independent, professional membership organization established to promote excellence, integrity, and innovation in the fields of training, coaching, leadership development, performance consulting, and lifelong learning.

Our Global Functions

ASTC functions as a global professional community and standards organization that:

  • Defines professional competency benchmarks for trainers and coaches
  • Accredits certification programs and learning providers
  • Promotes ethical conduct and professional accountability
  • Supports career development and global mobility of professionals
  • Facilitates knowledge sharing, research, and best practices
  • Encourages continuous learning and professional recognition

Our Diverse Community

Our members include practitioners from across the global learning ecosystem:

  • Corporate Trainers
  • L&D Professionals
  • Executive and Business Coaches
  • Academic Trainers and Instructional Designers
  • Leadership Development Specialists
  • Hr & Talent Development Professionals
  • Independent Consultants and Facilitators
  • Training Institutions and Corporate Learning Teams

ASTC operates with a global mindset while maintaining alignment with international education standards, workforce development frameworks, and professional competency models. Our mission is to ensure that training and coaching professionals are equipped with the skills, credibility, and ethical standards required to thrive in a rapidly evolving global economy.

Purpose & Direction

Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision

To be a globally respected professional society recognized for developing competent, ethical, and future-ready trainers and coaches who positively transform individuals, organizations, and societies through impactful learning and professional excellence.

Our Mission

To strengthen the global training and coaching profession by setting international standards, enabling credible certification, promoting ethics and continuous professional development, fostering innovation and partnerships, and empowering professionals to deliver measurable learning impact and organizational performance.
