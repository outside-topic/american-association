Professional Standards

ASTC Competency Framework

The ASTC Competency Framework defines the professional standards, skills architecture, and performance expectations for trainers, coaches, learning professionals, facilitators, and leadership development practitioners.

This framework establishes a structured, measurable, and globally aligned model for professional capability development, certification validation, career progression, and workforce readiness.

ASTC’s competency model integrates

Technical competencies

Behavioral competencies

Ethical standards

Leadership capabilities

Digital and innovation skills

Assessment and evaluation rigor

Industry relevance

The framework ensures that certified professionals demonstrate not only knowledge, but real-world application, professional conduct, and measurable performance impact.

Capability Architecture

ASTC Competency Model

The ASTC Competency Model is a multi-layered professional capability architecture that defines how competencies evolve across career stages and professional roles.

Core Dimensions

Knowledge & Domain Expertise
Practical Application & Delivery Excellence
Learning Design & Facilitation
Coaching & Behavioral Change
Assessment & Measurement
Digital Enablement
Ethical Governance
Leadership & Influence
Business Impact & Value Creation
