Membership – American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC)

Membership with the American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC) represents a professional commitment to excellence, ethical practice, continuous development, and global professional recognition in the fields of training, coaching, facilitation, leadership development, and organizational learning.

ASTC membership provides individuals and institutions with a credible professional identity, access to globally aligned competency frameworks, and certification pathways

Knowledge resources, professional networking, and career development opportunities through our global network of experts.

By becoming a member of ASTC, professionals join a trusted international community dedicated to advancing learning impact, workforce capability, and professional standards worldwide.

Professional Standards

What is Membership

ASTC Membership is a formal professional affiliation that recognizes individuals and organizations who align with the Society’s professional standards, ethical code, and competency frameworks.

Membership signifies:

  • Commitment to professional excellence and lifelong learning
  • Adherence to ethical standards and responsible practice
  • Recognition within a global professional community
  • Access to professional development and certification pathways
  • Engagement in knowledge sharing and industry collaboration

Career Stage Support

ASTC membership supports professionals across multiple career stages — from emerging trainers to senior industry leaders — offering structured pathways for progression, recognition, and leadership.

Requirements

Membership Eligibility

Eligibility is based on education, professional experience, ethical commitment, and professional engagement.

Detailed eligibility criteria are defined for each membership category to ensure the highest standards of professional practice.

General eligibility includes:

Relevant education or professional training

Demonstrated experience in training, coaching, or learning roles

Commitment to ASTC Code of Conduct

Willingness to maintain CPD compliance

Professional references (for higher tiers)
