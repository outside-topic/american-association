Trust & Transparency

Secure Credential Verification Platform

The ASTC Verification & Registry Platform provides a secure, transparent, and reliable system for validating professional credentials, membership status, and certification authenticity. This platform enables employers, clients, institutions, regulators, and the public to confirm the legitimacy of ASTC-certified professionals and accredited members, ensuring trust, integrity, and accountability across the professional ecosystem.

Platform Capabilities

Verification Infrastructure

ASTC's verification infrastructure supports digital credentialing, fraud prevention, international recognition, and real-time status validation.

Digital Credentialing

Secure digital certificates with blockchain verification and QR code authentication.

Fraud Prevention

Advanced security measures to prevent credential forgery and unauthorized use.

Global Recognition

Internationally recognized credentials accepted across borders and industries.

Real-Time Validation

Instant verification results with up-to-date credential status information.
