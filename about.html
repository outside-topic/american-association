About the Society

A Unified Professional Ecosystem

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches was established to create a unified professional ecosystem for trainers, coaches, facilitators, instructional designers, and organizational development practitioners.

The Society exists to:

  • Promote globally benchmarked professional competency standards
  • Strengthen ethical governance and professional accountability
  • Partnering for structured certification and career pathways
  • Encourage lifelong learning and professional excellence
  • Support workforce capability development and organizational performance
  • Foster international collaboration and knowledge exchange

Global Standard

Unified Professional Framework

ASTC is guided by internationally recognized frameworks in adult learning, leadership development, coaching psychology, performance management, digital learning, and human capital development. Our standards reflect real-world industry needs, emerging technologies, and evolving workforce demands.

The Society is member-driven, practitioner-focused, and future-oriented — ensuring relevance for professionals across corporate, academic, consulting, and entrepreneurial environments.

Ethics & Standards

Code of Conduct

ASTC upholds a comprehensive Code of Conduct that governs the ethical behavior and professional responsibility of all members, certified professionals, partners, and accredited institutions.

Core Principles Include:

Professional integrity and honesty

Respect for learners, clients, and stakeholders

Confidentiality and data protection

Competence and continuous development

Non-discrimination and inclusivity

Responsible marketing and representation

Compliance with laws and professional standards

Avoidance of conflicts of interest

Violations of the Code of Conduct are addressed through transparent investigation and disciplinary procedures to maintain professional credibility and public trust.
