Ethics & Professional Standards – American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC)

The American Society for Trainers and Coaches (ASTC) is committed to upholding the highest standards of professional integrity, ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability across all members, certified professionals, partners, and accredited institutions.

Our Ethics and Professional Standards framework establishes clear expectations for professional behavior, quality assurance, learner protection, and public trust. It ensures that ASTC-members operate responsibly, ethically, and in alignment with international best practices.

Role-Specific Standards

Conduct & Responsibility

Trainer Conduct Policy

The Trainer Conduct Policy defines behavioral expectations for training professionals.

Key Expectations:

  • Professional demeanor and respect
  • Accurate representation of credentials
  • Fair treatment of learners
  • Objective assessment practices
  • Intellectual property respect
  • Inclusive learning environments
  • Continuous professional improvement
  • Compliance with ASTC standards

Coach Responsibility Charter

The Coach Responsibility Charter defines ethical obligations specific to coaching practice.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Client confidentiality and trust
  • Ethical contracting and scope clarity
  • Professional boundaries
  • Competency-based practice
  • Supervision and reflective practice
  • Cultural sensitivity
  • Avoidance of dependency
  • Outcome-focused coaching
