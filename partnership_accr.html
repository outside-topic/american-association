Institutional Accreditation

Institutional Accreditation recognizes organizations that demonstrate excellence in governance, learning quality, assessment integrity, ethical compliance, and professional development capability.

  • Organizational governance and leadership
  • Academic and learning governance
  • Faculty qualification standards
  • Curriculum and competency alignment
  • Assessment quality systems
  • Learner protection policies
  • Data privacy and compliance
  • Continuous improvement mechanisms

Accredited institutions gain formal recognition as ASTC-approved professional education providers.

Training Provider Accreditation

Training Provider Accreditation validates training organizations that deliver ASTC-aligned certification and professional development programs.

  • Learner experience standards
