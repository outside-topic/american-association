Honorary President

Ron Thomas

Ron Thomas is a globally recognized executive advisor and leadership strategist with more than two decades of experience architecting enterprise-level people, leadership, and workforce transformations across the Middle East, Africa, North America, and Asia.

Known for pioneering the Workforce Architect approach, Ron works at the intersection of enterprise strategy, organizational design, leadership capability, and future-ready workforce systems. His advisory work supports CEOs, Boards, and CHROs in translating strategy into execution through modern workforce architecture, leadership pipelines, governance models, and operating structures aligned to growth, productivity, risk, and resilience.

A respected global thought leader, Ron has been consistently recognized among the world’s most influential HR and leadership voices, including Top 5 HR Thinkers in the Middle East (CIPD) and Asia’s 50 Most Talented Global HR Leaders. He serves as Executive Faculty at Howard University’s Global HR Leadership Institute and is a member of the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council.

Advancing the art, science, and practice of professional coaching globally through standard-setting and community building.
